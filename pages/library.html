Math Library

A collection of fundamental study cards to refresh your core math concepts.

Basic Math

Addition (+)

Concept: Combining two or more numbers together to find their total sum.

Example:

If you have 5 apples and buy 3 more:

5 + 3 = 8
Basic Math

Subtraction (-)

Concept: Taking one number away from another to find the difference between them.

Example:

If you have 10 cookies and eat 4:

10 - 4 = 6
Basic Math

Multiplication (×)

Concept: Repeated addition of the same number a specified number of times.

Example:

4 groups with 3 items in each group:

4 × 3 = 12
Basic Math

Division (÷)

Concept: Splitting a number into equal parts or groups.

Example:

Sharing 12 slices of pizza equally among 4 friends:

12 ÷ 4 = 3
